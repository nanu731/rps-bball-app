# Private byte delivery and optional offline clips

Adopted2026-10-12 after five independent Council analyses, five fresh anonymous
reviews and chairman synthesis. Use one private immutable-file lifecycle for
practice clips and scouting PDFs: reserve capacity, quarantine upload, trusted
byte validation, exact-version approval or eligible-author bypass, then live
authorized retrieval. Begin with the existing PDF contract in ADR0003. The
provisional clip limits are MP4/H.264 with optional AAC audio,10 MiB,30 seconds
and at most1920×1080; these are development bounds, not measured workload or
Free-plan feasibility. Reject unsupported files without silent compression.

Validation and approval bind the immutable object ID, authoritative length/hash,
team and play/game target. Recheck current authority at each command, finalization,
approval/publication and retrieval. One active upload per account initially;
atomic aggregate storage/transfer admission includes reservations, pending,
rejected, interrupted and retained versions. Expiry does not free occupied bytes.
Stable operation IDs and explicit reconciliation handle uncertain replies without
duplicate publication, guessed receipts, automatic deletion or blind replay.
Bound trusted parsing/decoding resources and concurrency; unvalidated files cannot
publish. Client MIME, titles, sizes and success reports are never authoritative.

Fetch a complete clip through an authenticated, budgeted delivery boundary into
an account/team/version-scoped temporary local file before AVPlayer playback.
This deliberately trades initial download latency for a simpler authorization
and transfer boundary; native playback does not independently issue bearer-link
or overlapping remote range requests. Verify the actual delivery/player path.
Retries that transfer bytes again consume allowance; uncertain delivery stays
conservatively charged. Reuse verified saved files to avoid accidental downloads.

“Keep on this device” explicitly retains a complete, integrity-checked file and
restart-safe manifest after space checks. Incomplete files cannot play. Sign-out,
account change and known revocation block access through the app while preserving
files; no silent eviction. Previously authorized retained clips may play offline
when authority cannot be refreshed, with that limitation visible. Delivered copies
cannot reliably be recalled; every future remote operation checks current authority.
Approval remains per user/capability, with no per-download review. This is optional
clip retention, not full offline synchronization of shared statistics.

Develop and test locally now. Hosted delivery stays unavailable until actual
organization capacity/other-project use, separate transfer/request allowances,
validator hosting/resources, provider rights and enforceable strict$0 protection
are verified. An app ledger alone cannot establish provider billing safety.
Unknown/stale capacity fails closed; exhausted capacity preserves input/history
and explains recovery. No paid fallback, full-film hosting or shared photo library.
